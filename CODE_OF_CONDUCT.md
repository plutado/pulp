# Code of conduct

Pulp is a small open-source project. Everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, is expected to behave as a good colleague would.

## Expected

- Be direct about the work and kind about the person. Criticise code, designs and decisions; do not criticise people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding.
- Welcome newcomers. A question answered in the open helps the next person too.
- Respect that people have different backgrounds, experience and amounts of time to give.

## Not accepted

- Insults, demeaning remarks, harassment or intimidation of any kind, including about someone's identity, background or way of speaking.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, repeating a settled point, or badgering a contributor.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, David Fugate, is responsible for this code and may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break it, and may block anyone who keeps breaking it. Report a problem privately by email to the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or through GitHub's report feature on the offending content. Reports are read, kept confidential, and answered within a week.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project runs, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
